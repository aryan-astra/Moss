MOSS 1.3.0 — MUSIC AND PLAY

Exit the older Moss from its tray menu. Extract ALL files and run Moss.exe.
No separate .NET runtime installation is required.

New: browser/default-output level fallback; clickable music card with optional
song details/artwork/timeline; species CD/cassette/radio/headphones; music notes,
rare optional compliments, petting hearts, less compressed dance and real hops;
a gentler movement/shock-based twig grip and body lift; temporary football play;
explicit Save changes alongside immediate preference persistence.

Output-level fallback is not recording or song recognition. It may react to
other sound and may miss non-default/exclusive/very quiet output. Metadata needs
a cooperating Windows session. The music card never invents missing details.

95 Core tests and four Wine component checks passed. Renderer evidence includes
42 poses and a simulated-input motion exercise. Native Windows audio/device,
mixed-DPI, scheduler and long-run performance acceptance remains unverified.

Read README.md for the current guide, architecture snapshot and research
 status. Neural import/vision are researched proposals, not shipped
features. The full premium-art/100-moment master brief is not claimed complete.

Existing notes remain in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Moss\notebook. Preserve this folder.
After moving the executable, update enabled startup/closed-app reminder paths
by toggling those settings off/on.
